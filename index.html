From farm to cup

Where it grows

High in the hills of Huila, Colombia

Our beans come from twelve family farms on the slopes of the Andes. Cool nights at altitude slow the ripening of the cherry, which gives the coffee more sweetness and a cleaner taste.

  • 1,700 mAltitude
  • 12Family farms
  • 18 °CAverage day

The harvest

Picked by hand, only when red

From October to December, pickers pass by the same tree up to five times. They take only the ripe red cherries and leave the green ones for the next round.

  • 5×Passes per tree
  • Oct–DecSeason

Washing and drying

Three weeks in the sun

The cherries are pulped and washed the same day. The beans then dry slowly on raised beds and are turned by hand several times a day, until only 11% of their moisture is left.

  • 21 daysDrying
  • 11%Final moisture

The roast

Roasted in small batches, shipped within a week

We roast 12 kg at a time and follow each batch by ear and by colour. The beans rest for two days, then go into bags with the roast date and the farm printed on them.

  • 12 kgPer batch
  • 11–14 minRoast time
  • 7 daysRoast to door
